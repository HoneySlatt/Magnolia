# Reference projects

The two sessions on which the budgets of the sound are measured, one for each
[tier](ROADMAP.md#hardware). The owner adopted them on 2026-11-11, at M1.3; they are never tuned
for afterwards: a figure that misses its floor is a fault of Magnolia, not of the project. A change to a project is an ADR, and
every figure taken on the old one is marked as such.

Neither project can play yet. They become test sessions when M1.4 plays sessions from disk, gain
their plugins at M1.6 and M1.7, and become Magnolia projects at Stage 2.

## Common to both

- 48 kHz, a song of 3 min 30 s at 140 BPM in 4/4, played in a loop for the runs of eight hours.
- Audio files are 24-bit WAV, generated by a script from a fixed seed: no sample library is
  downloaded, and anyone can build the same files. Their content loads the disk and the plugins as
  music does: sustained and percussive material, silence between takes.
- MIDI parts are generated the same way: chords, a bass line, drums, a lead with pitch bends.
- Every plugin runs out of the engine's process, as Magnolia always runs them
  ([ADR-0003](adr/0003-every-plugin-out-of-the-engines-process.md)). Windows plugins run under the
  system's Wine, in a test prefix.
- Plugins are named with their version on the day of the first measure, and their settings are
  saved in the project.

On the HP Laptop 17-ca2xxx, at 128 frames, on mains power. It is provisional until the laptop
itself is measured ([ADR-0014](adr/0014-the-quality-bar-measured-against-the-market.md)); it must
fit within 2.5 GB, measured at M1.9.
